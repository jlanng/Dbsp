B2, paced bulk loads: the runs made before the pacing was built, to find what stops a
leader during a large load (docs/design/enrichment-service.md, S9, "Paced bulk loads").
The scale rig with --load-only (one node, the table loaded through its writer in
versions of 20,000 rows, nothing after), on this machine: 4 cores, its memory moving
between 15.6 and 11.6 GB total while the runs were made, the connector stack up (about
3 GB of it). Made with the rig's load loop changed by hand for each experiment
(SCALE_LOAD_EXPERIMENT; not committed); "none" is the code before pacing.

What the collector logged (the rig's GcLog: every gen2 with the runtime's reason and
kind, every pause over 200 ms; d5m-none every collection):

- The long young pauses are copies. Under eight server heaps the young generations
  filled to gigabytes of rows that survive, and one collection copied them: in d5m-none
  a gen1 with 3,376 MB in it took 2,444 ms; in b4m-1 a gen1 took 1,374 ms. Waiting
  between commits (sleep) changes nothing: the collector runs on bytes allocated, not
  on time. Asking for a young collection every step of allocation bounds the copy, and
  gen1 rather than gen0 (with gen0 the survivors piled up in gen1 instead).
- The multi-second stops at five million are blocking compacting gen2s the collector
  chose because the machine's memory load stood at its high-load threshold (reason 0,
  AllocSmall, kind 0: d5m-none at load 11,321 of 11,321 MB, 3,772 ms; e-gc1-256's own
  induced gen1 turned into a 3,524 ms gen2 at 10,768 of 11,140 MB). The table is
  6.5 GB live; the machine had about 3 GB in other processes and 11 to 12 GB in all
  while these ran. No pace changes what the table needs, and a collection asked for
  there is the collector's cue to make the full one.
- A full compacting collection of the loaded 5M table costs 2.1 to 2.3 s on these four
  cores whenever it happens (the rig's own forced collections after each load).
- The large-object heap gains ~84 MB/s of garbage during the load: the table log's
  change records (each chunk up to MaxRecordBytes, copied to an array of its own,
  ~900 KB) and the chunk buffer's growth. It is collected by the background gen2s
  (reason 4) and reached 933 MB before the full collection in d5m-none. Not changed
  in this step.

The pacing that was built (TableLoadPacing: a gen1 every 128 MB once a run passes
100,000 changes, nothing above 85 % of the high-load threshold) was then measured
before and after: 2026-11-16-b2-paced-load.tsv, its collector log
2026-11-16-b2-paced-load.gc.log.
